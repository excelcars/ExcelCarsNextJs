# Customer Management Migration - Gap Analysis and Improvement Backlog

## Comparison basis

- Legacy SSOT: [customers.md](docs/module/existing_ssot/customers.md)
- Current Next.js SSOT: [customers.md](customers.md)

The supplied brief calls the module “User Access” in places, but the named legacy module and Next.js route are Customer Management (`Configuration/CustomerManagement` and `/config/customers`). This comparison therefore covers Customers. This file is intentionally separate from the current-state SSOT and does not authorize implementation.

## Intentional improvements or differences to retain unless business asks otherwise

- Server-side access checks for the Customer Management page/actions are stronger than the legacy role-4 UI-only restriction.
- Debounced URL-driven broad search, deterministic newest-first order, immediate listing, pagination, and delete confirmation are usability improvements over the legacy postback screen.
- Prisma foreign-key protection prevents deletion of referenced customers instead of attempting a delete and relying on legacy exception handling.

## Recommended implementation order

1. Confirm the role-4 policy and canonical customer/contact requirements; implement server-side authorization and validation.
2. Fix quick-create taxonomy/duplicate behavior and make failed deletion visible.
3. Add export and any approved field-specific lookup/autocomplete requirements.
4. Improve list detail, page clamping, and observability as follow-up work.

Do not merge this backlog into `customers.md` or delete this document without explicit approval.
